About Us

We built this application to help food insecure families all across the world. With rising poverty rates and world hunger, we are committed to solving food insecurity through technology.

Our Mission

Every day, restaurants and cafeterias throw away tons of surplus food while families go hungry just a few miles away. FoodRescue closes that gap — giving donors a dead-simple way to post available food and letting shelters, food banks, and individuals claim it before the window closes.

How It Works

  • 🍽️ Donors post surplus food with a pickup window and location.
  • 🏠 Recipients browse available listings and claim what they need.
  • ⏰ Posts expire automatically when the pickup window closes — no clutter.
  • 🔑 No accounts needed — donors get a 4-digit PIN to manage their post.

Built With ❤️ for Good

FoodRescue was built at a hackathon with one goal: get food to people who need it as fast as possible. We believe technology should solve real problems, not create new ones. This is ours.
